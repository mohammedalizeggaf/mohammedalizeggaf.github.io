Who am I?

Hello, I'm Mohammed Ali.

I'm a web and mobile developer from Tangier, Morocco. I build full-stack web applications with PHP and JavaScript, and native Android apps with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

I learn by shipping. At SEBN-MA, I rebuilt an internal platform that manages users and software licences. For Top Software, I developed an after-sales service app with ticketing and an admin panel. And I designed, built and published Sobhaty, my own app, on Google Play.

Today I work as a freelance developer while continuing my studies. Whatever the project, I care about the same three things: clean code, fast pages and interfaces that feel simple.
