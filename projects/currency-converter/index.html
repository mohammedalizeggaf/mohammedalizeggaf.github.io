/Currency Converter

Android converter for the US dollar, euro, Algerian dinar and Moroccan dirham that updates as you type.

A small Android app to convert between four currencies: US dollar, euro, Algerian dinar and Moroccan dirham. The result is recalculated on every keystroke, and custom listeners keep track of the selected currency pair.

#highlights

  • Instant conversion while typing
  • Four currencies: USD, EUR, DZD and MAD
